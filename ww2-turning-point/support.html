How to play

  1. Pick a war. On the title screen, choose Britain 1936 · story campaign, Quick start · 3 September 1939, or New game. New game lets you play as the United Kingdom, Germany, the Soviet Union or Italy.
  2. Start the clock. Tap ▶ to start the war. Speed is set with 1×, 2×, 5× or 8×. Fronts, news and headlines appear on the map as the war unfolds.
  3. Give orders. Use the rail on the left and the round Support button:
    • Forces moves your army groups;
    • Support sends air and naval support to a territory;
    • Diplomacy handles peace, alliances and neutrality;
    • Allies answers your allies' requests.
    Order sheets show a forecast before you confirm. Hold sends nothing.

Questions

Does the game need an account or the internet?

No. There is no sign-in, and the game runs offline.

Where is my save?

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Resume campaign on the title screen continues it. Deleting the app deletes your save.

Can I play in portrait?

No. The iPhone app plays in landscape only.

The war is too hard or too easy.

Choose Easy, Normal or Hard on the title screen before you start a war. Normal is the default.

What does "alt-history" mean on some cards?

Some of your choices lead away from what really happened, for example a separate peace, changing sides, or the story's "dictator path". The game marks these as alt-history: they are fiction. Choices marked "follows history" match the real events.

Is it historically accurate?

The map, dates and story beats are based on cited sources, and you can open those sources from the story cards. The war itself is a simulation. Once you give orders, the outcome can differ from history.
